Browser Control

Your Chrome.
Ocean's capabilities.

Read pages, fill in forms and manage tabs through chat. Your website logins stay in your regular Chrome profile.

Getting started

Connect
in two steps

Start in Plugins → Browser control in Ocean.

  1. 01

    Install the extension

    Click Install extension in Ocean and follow the instructions in Chrome.

  2. 02

    Connect to Ocean

    Return to the app and click Connect. Then choose which sites to allow.

You set the limits

Tabs under your control

Ocean opens separate tabs for each chat. To hand over a personal tab you already have open, use the extension button in Chrome.

Connecting alone does not give Ocean access to every site. You can change permissions in the app.
